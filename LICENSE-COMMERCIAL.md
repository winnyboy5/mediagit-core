# Commercial Licensing

MediaGit is **source available** under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE).
It is not open source, and this page explains exactly where the line falls, so you
can tell in a minute whether you need to talk to us.

## You do not need a commercial licence

The Additional Use Grant in `LICENSE` permits **production use, at any scale, by
any organisation**. Specifically, all of the following are free:

- Running MediaGit to version **your own** media — films, games, campaigns,
  archives, whatever it is you make.
- Self-hosting `mediagit-server` on your own infrastructure or your own cloud
  account, for your own team.
- Any team size, any company size, any revenue. There is **no seat cap and no
  size cap**.
- Reading, modifying, forking and redistributing the source.
- Building tools, plugins and integrations on top of it, and selling those.
- Consulting, deploying, configuring or supporting MediaGit for a client, where
  the client runs their own instance.
- Non-production use of every kind — evaluation, development, testing, CI,
  research, teaching.

If you are a studio, a startup, a freelancer or an enterprise using MediaGit for
your own work, you are covered. You owe nothing and need to ask no one.

## You do need a commercial licence

One thing is reserved:

> Offering the Licensed Work to third parties **on a hosted or embedded basis in
> order to compete with the Licensor's paid version(s)** of the Licensed Work.

In plain terms, you need a commercial licence to:

- Run MediaGit **as a service for other people** — a hosted "MediaGit cloud",
  a managed offering, or MediaGit rented out per-seat to customers who are not
  your own organisation.
- **Embed** MediaGit inside a product you sell where MediaGit's versioning,
  dedup or delta engine is what the customer is paying for.

The distinction is not "are you making money" — it is "are you selling MediaGit
itself to third parties". Using it commercially for your own work is free. Reselling
it as a service is not.

If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, ask. The answer is usually
"you are fine", and it costs nothing to check.

## What we sell

Rather than charging for the right to self-host, the commercial offering is:

- **MediaGit Cloud** — hosted, managed, backed up, with no infrastructure to run.
- **Premium features** — SSO/SAML, fine-grained RBAC, audit logging, compliance
  reporting, priority support and an SLA.
- **Commercial licences** for the hosted/embedded use reserved above.

## Eventual open source

Every released version converts to the **Change License — AGPL-3.0-or-later —
four years after that version is published**, automatically, under the terms of
`LICENSE`. Each version carries its own clock. Nothing is withheld permanently.
